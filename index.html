01 — The Problem

Most Portfolios Overpay on 15–30% of Every Invoice

Fuel surcharges at non-contracted rates. Environmental fees applied without basis. Overages triggered by definitions that don't match your actual contract. It compounds quietly for years.

02 — Our Edge

Invoice-Level Forensics, Not High-Level Summaries

We go line by line. Rate structures, service frequencies, weight thresholds, escalation clauses. Then we build a vendor-ready dispute package that recovers real money — without litigation.

03 — The Audit

Audit Before You Pay Another Dollar

We reconcile every invoice against your contracted terms. What we find either saves you money immediately or validates that your vendor is actually performing. Either outcome has value.

04 — The Fix

Governance Built to Scale With Your Portfolio

We don't just fix what's broken today. We build governance frameworks — rate cap provisions, service verification requirements, dispute protocols — that protect you as you add sites.
