// The Methodology

Audit before paying. Fix before scaling.

Two non-negotiable phases that protect your portfolio from compounding leakage as you grow.

Phase 01

Audit Before Paying

  • Invoice-level reconciliation against contracted rates, service frequencies, and actual weights
  • Overage & excess charge identification — fuel surcharges, environmental fees, and administrative markups outside contract scope
  • Rate benchmarking against regional market data to flag above-market pricing
  • Credit recovery for duplicate billing, missed service credits, and retroactive overcharges
  • Dispute documentation with vendor-ready evidence packages to recover funds owed

Most portfolios are overpaying on 15-30% of invoices. The audit finds it. The documentation proves it.

Phase 02

Fix Before Scaling

  • Contract renegotiation with enforceable language on rate caps, service guarantees, and termination rights
  • Governance framework setup — approval hierarchies, invoice routing, and escalation protocols
  • Vendor performance benchmarks built into contracts before renewal or expansion
  • Portfolio standardization across service agreements to eliminate one-off terms that create exposure
  • Scaling playbook — processes that hold as you add sites without adding proportional overhead

Scaling broken contracts multiplies the problem. Fix the structure first, then grow with control.

// Our Approach

Governance that proves control

A disciplined, repeatable methodology built for portfolios that cannot afford to scale broken processes.

Total Cost Behavior Analysis

We look at waste spend as a total cost behavior — not just line items. That means analyzing rate structures, service frequency patterns, overage triggers, and contractual escalation clauses across your entire vendor portfolio simultaneously.

Most operators audit invoices. We audit the relationship between your contract terms and your operational reality — which is where the real leakage lives.

Contract Governance Framework

// GOVERNANCE COMPONENTS
Rate Cap Provisions — Contractual limits on annual rate increases, tied to CPI or fixed percentages with vendor-side audit rights.
Service Verification Requirements — Invoice acceptance conditional on verifiable service completion data — not vendor self-reporting.
Dispute Resolution Protocols — Pre-defined escalation paths, documentation standards, and credit timelines that remove ambiguity from recoveries.
Termination & Transition Rights — Exit provisions that protect your portfolio if service quality or pricing drift beyond contracted parameters.
